Privacy policy

Built to keep your work on your device.

Effective July 30, 2026

In plain language: Blueprint Studio has no advertising, analytics, telemetry, or CoreNucApps cloud account. Your drawings and imported technical data are not automatically sent to CoreNucApps.

Information the application handles

Blueprint Studio opens and saves drawing projects, templates, spreadsheets, source-code files, database metadata, exported documents, and local settings at your direction. The application may also handle server addresses, database user names, database passwords, and AWS identity information while performing an import you request.

Information sent to CoreNucApps

Blueprint Studio does not include advertising, analytics, telemetry, or a CoreNucApps cloud account. It does not automatically send drawings, source code, spreadsheet data, database metadata, credentials, usage information, or local diagnostic logs to CoreNucApps.

User-requested network connections

When you choose an AWS import, Blueprint Studio connects directly to Amazon Web Services through the AWS SDK and the credentials configured by you or your organization. When you choose database mapping, Blueprint Studio connects directly to the database server you select. Those services may process connection and usage information under their own terms and privacy policies. CoreNucApps does not receive those credentials or imported results.

Credentials

Database passwords and connection strings are retained in memory only for the requested operation and are not written into Blueprint project files, recent-file lists, or diagnostics. Blueprint Studio does not request or store AWS access keys; AWS credentials remain managed by the AWS SDK and your configured identity provider.

Local diagnostics and recovery

The application writes local diagnostic logs and autosave recovery information to your local application-data folder. Logs are retained for approximately 14 days and contain application, runtime, and error information. Drawing content and credentials are not intentionally included. These files remain on your device unless you choose to share them.

Data control and deletion

You control where drawing projects and exports are saved and can delete those files through Windows. Uninstalling the application removes installed program files. User-created documents and local application data may remain so an uninstall does not destroy your work; you can remove the local CoreNucApps Blueprint Studio application-data folder manually.

Children

Blueprint Studio is a general productivity application and is not directed to children. The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

CoreNucApps may update this policy when the application's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
