CoreNuc Auction App — Lazy Lions Edition

Purpose-built for in-person auctions, the Lazy Lions Edition keeps events organized from seller intake through bidding, payment, item release, seller payout, and final closeout.

Get Support

About the App

CoreNuc Auction App — Lazy Lions Edition gives auction organizers and staff one central Windows application for preparing catalogs, documenting buyer eligibility, recording live results, collecting payments, releasing purchased items, settling with consignors, and closing out an event.

Run a simple auction from one computer or connect several workstations over a local network for registration, live auction entry, and checkout. Internet access is not required for normal auction-night operation.

Key Features

Auction Setup

Create events with the date, location, buyer's premium, and sales-tax rate, then monitor auction-night progress from the dashboard.

Catalog & Consignors

Register sellers, search and archive records, bulk-create lots from photos, track category, condition, dimensions, and storage, then print catalogs, intake sheets, or labels.

Buyer Registration & Agreements

Assign bidder numbers, capture terms and signature confirmation, record ID and deposit status, attach tax certificates, apply bidding restrictions, and print bidder documents.

Live Auction & Command Mode

Record results with configurable keyboard shortcuts or a focused command screen showing the current lot, photo, next lot, connection state, backup state, and last result.

Checkout, Receipts & Pickup

Find buyers by number or contact details, calculate charges, record payments and refunds, preview or save PDF receipts, and track paid items through pickup or shipping release.

Seller Settlements

Calculate consignor proceeds and commissions, add approved deductions, and track settlement approval and payment.

Multi-Computer Sync

Use one host and multiple local-network workstations so auction staff can work together without relying on the internet.

Staff Access & Audit

Protect sensitive actions with staff roles, PINs, manager approvals, record locks, and a searchable audit history.

Reports, Analytics & Accounting

Export operational reports, accounting-ready CSV schedules, and analytics for sell-through, bidder conversion, checkout time, category performance, and unsold inventory aging.

Communications Center

Prepare registration confirmations, invoices, receipts, balance reminders, pickup notices, and settlement statements, then preserve the staff-reviewed communication history.

Privacy & Customer Data

Set retention periods, record communication consent, export a customer's information, and anonymize eligible archived records with manager approval.

Encrypted Backup & Recovery

Protect verified backups with AES-256 authenticated encryption and use auction-night recovery guidance after an interrupted session or host replacement.

Accessible Windows Experience

Responsive, scroll-safe layouts support keyboard operation, visible focus, screen readers, Windows contrast themes, display scaling, and text-based status indicators.

Who It Is For

  • Local and community auction houses
  • Charity and fundraising auction organizers
  • Estate, consignment, and benefit auction teams
  • Cashiers, clerks, auctioneers, and event managers
  • Organizations that need a dependable offline-capable auction workflow
