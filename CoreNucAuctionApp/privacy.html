Privacy Policy

CoreNuc Auction App — Lazy Lions Edition

Effective date: July 28, 2026

Overview

CoreNuc Auction App — Lazy Lions Edition is a Windows application for managing in-person auctions. It is designed to keep auction and customer information under the control of the auction business operating the app.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the application may store, how that information is used, when it may leave a device, and what choices users have. It applies to CoreNuc Auction App — Lazy Lions Edition and this supporting website.

Information Stored by the App

The application may store auction-related information in a local database and local configuration files on each computer where it is installed. Depending on what the user enters, this may include:

  • Auction names, dates, locations, buyer's premium rates, sales-tax rates, and event status
  • Consignor names, seller numbers, phone numbers, email addresses, postal addresses, commission rates, and payment status
  • Bidder names, bidder numbers, phone numbers, email addresses, postal addresses, and tax-exempt status
  • Buyer terms acceptance, signature confirmation, deposit and card-on-file status, ID-verification status, bidding restrictions, internal registration notes, and tax-exemption certificates
  • Lot numbers, descriptions, categories, condition, dimensions, storage location, seller assignments, reserves, result status, winning bidder numbers, hammer prices, completion times, and local photo file paths
  • Invoices, charges, discounts, taxes, payment methods, payment amounts, payment references, refunds, balances, and finalization status
  • Consignor settlement calculations, deductions, approval status, payout methods, and payout references
  • Cash-drawer opening amounts, expected cash, counted cash, reconciliation results, and workstation identifiers
  • Pickup and shipping plans, storage locations, release recipient, ID-check status, confirmation or tracking details, release date, and releasing staff member
  • Communication-consent grants and withdrawals, purposes, sources, prepared customer messages, recipient addresses, delivery status, and communication history
  • Staff display names, usernames, roles, permissions, account status, protected PIN credentials, and sign-in activity
  • Audit records describing sales, payments, corrections, approvals, imports, security changes, and other actions
  • Application, printing, networking, backup, update, and organization settings

The app does not require a CoreNucApps cloud account to use its normal local auction-management features.

How Information Is Used

Information stored by the application is used to provide requested auction-management functions, including:

  • Creating auction events and tracking auction progress
  • Managing consignors, lots, reserves, bidders, and tax-exempt status
  • Recording winning bidders, hammer prices, passed lots, withdrawals, no-sales, returns, and buyer transfers
  • Calculating buyer's premiums, sales tax, invoice totals, balances, commissions, deductions, and consignor proceeds
  • Recording payments, refunds, cash-drawer activity, invoice finalization, settlement approval, and seller payouts
  • Documenting buyer agreements, registration eligibility, tax-exemption evidence, and bidding restrictions
  • Tracking paid purchases through pickup or shipping release
  • Preparing customer-requested or consented communications and recording their outcome
  • Printing labels, bidder cards, agreements, catalogs, intake sheets, receipts, and consignor statements
  • Creating reports, analytics, accounting schedules, customer-data exports, backups, import previews, audit exports, closeout packages, and support diagnostics
  • Synchronizing auction operations between computers when the user enables local-network features
  • Enforcing staff permissions, manager approvals, record locks, and audit history

CoreNucApps does not use locally stored auction data for targeted advertising, behavioral profiling, or sale to third parties.

Local Storage, Backups, and Restore

Auction data is normally stored locally on the computer. The app may automatically create verified local backups and may let an authorized user create, copy, or restore a backup.

Authorized administrators can enable password-protected backups. New backup files then use AES-256 authenticated encryption. The password is protected on the configured Windows computer, but the auction business must retain it securely and separately for restoration on a replacement computer.

Backup and closeout packages may contain the auction database and therefore may include bidder, consignor, agreement attachment, lot, invoice, payment, pickup, consent, communication, settlement, staff, and audit information. Users control where manually copied backups and final closeout packages are saved.

If a user copies a backup to a USB drive, network location, cloud-synchronized folder, email, or another service, that destination may process or retain the file according to its own settings and privacy policy.

When a backup is restored, the app may create a safety copy of the current database before replacing it.

Imports, Exports, Printing, and Sharing

Authorized users may import auction data from CSV files, ZIP packages, or Excel workbooks. Imported files may contain auction, consignor, bidder, lot, sale, and payment records supplied by the user.

The app may produce CSV files, Excel workbooks, JSON customer-data exports, PDF reports or receipts, accounting schedules, analytics, statements, labels, bidder cards, buyer agreements, audit exports, encrypted backups, and closeout packages. These outputs may contain personal, transactional, or financial information.

Exporting, printing, copying, emailing, uploading, or otherwise sharing a file occurs at the user's direction. Users should review output before sharing it and provide it only to people and services authorized to receive the information.

Customer Communications and Consent

The Communications Center can prepare registration confirmations, winning-bid summaries, invoices, payment receipts, pickup reminders, unpaid-balance reminders, and consignor settlement statements using locally stored records.

The app opens a draft in the computer's default Windows email application or copies the message for staff. It does not send email itself. Staff review the content and choose whether to send it in the selected email application.

The app can keep an append-only history of communication-consent grants and withdrawals, including channel, purpose, source, time, staff member, and workstation. Prepared messages can be marked sent, failed, or cancelled for operational accountability.

The auction business is responsible for obtaining any consent required by law and for configuring and using its email, SMS, telephone, marketing, or other communication services lawfully.

Local-Network Synchronization

CoreNuc Auction App — Lazy Lions Edition can be used on one computer without synchronization. When an authorized user starts an Auction Host and connects other workstations, operational data and changes may travel between those computers over the local network.

Synchronization may include auctions, consignors, bidders, lots, sale results, invoices, payments, refunds, pickup releases, settlements, closeout status, and other operational changes needed to keep workstations consistent. Some sensitive attachments must be added or managed directly on the host.

This feature is intended for computers on a trusted private network. Users are responsible for controlling physical and network access, protecting the shared sync key, configuring the firewall appropriately, and stopping the host when synchronization is no longer needed.

Normal local-network synchronization does not require internet access. Users should not place the app's SQLite database in a shared network folder.

Staff Accounts, PINs, and Audit History

The app can store staff accounts, roles, permissions, account status, protected PIN credentials, and sign-in information. PINs are used to authenticate staff locally and to protect sensitive actions. Users should not share PINs or reuse important personal passwords as app PINs.

The Audit Center may retain information about who signed in, entered or corrected sales, handled payments, approved changes, changed staff access, or performed other protected actions. This history supports accountability, security review, and financial reconciliation.

Authorized users may export audit history. An exported audit file is controlled by the person or organization that saves and shares it.

Lot Photos and File Locations

Users may associate a photo with an auction lot. Supported images may be resized and copied into a local CatalogPhotos folder, and the app stores the resulting local file path.

Lot photo files are not automatically copied to other workstations through normal synchronization and are not included in database-only backups. Users are responsible for securing and separately backing up the CatalogPhotos folder and for managing any cloud or file-sharing service that processes those images.

Updates and Support Packages

Non-Store installations may let an administrator configure an optional HTTPS update feed. The hosting provider may receive standard network information such as IP address, request time, and software request details. Microsoft Store installations receive updates through Microsoft Store under Microsoft's applicable terms and privacy practices.

When an authorized user chooses to create a support package, the app produces diagnostic information about the application version, system configuration, database integrity, and synchronization status. The support package is designed to exclude auction database records, bidder and consignor details, payment records, PINs, and sync keys.

A support package is not automatically sent to CoreNucApps. The user controls whether and how it is shared.

Information the App Is Not Designed to Collect

CoreNuc Auction App — Lazy Lions Edition is not designed to collect or store:

  • Full payment-card numbers, card security codes, or online banking credentials
  • Government identification numbers
  • Precise device location
  • Contacts from the user's address book
  • Camera or microphone recordings
  • Advertising identifiers for targeted advertising

Payment references are free-form user entries intended for limited references such as a check number, transaction number, or the last four digits of a card. Users should never enter a full card number, security code, bank-account number, password, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Website Privacy

This supporting website is a static informational website providing product information, help content, screenshots, technology details, release information, and this Privacy Policy.

The website does not include account sign-in, payment processing, comments, or forms that intentionally collect personal information. If the site is hosted by a third-party platform, that platform may process standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, request logs, and security telemetry under its own policies.

Third-Party Services

The app may rely on Windows, installed printer drivers, user-selected file locations, spreadsheet or PDF readers, storage providers, update hosting, app distribution services, and other user-selected software for printing, opening, saving, copying, backing up, or sharing information.

CoreNucApps is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services that users choose to use with auction data or exported files.

Data Retention and Deletion

The auction business can configure a local record-retention period from 1 through 25 years based on its legal, accounting, tax, and operational duties.

Auction data remains in the local database until an authorized user edits, archives, deletes, or anonymizes it where supported, restores different data, removes the database, clears application data, or uninstalls the app and removes remaining data files.

Eligible archived bidder or consignor records may be anonymized only after a manager confirms retention and other legal obligations allow the action and provides an audit reason. Identifying customer fields are replaced while transaction totals and audit integrity are retained.

Some financial, agreement, consent, security, and audit records may be intentionally retained or protected from ordinary deletion to preserve transaction history and accountability. Closed auctions may become read-only unless an authorized manager reopens them with a recorded reason.

Automatic backups, manually saved backups, closeout packages, imported source files, exports, printed documents, lot photos, and files shared with other services must be managed separately in every location where they were saved or sent.

Security

CoreNuc Auction App — Lazy Lions Edition includes controls such as local staff authentication, role-based permissions, manager approvals, temporary record locks, append-only consent history, audit history, verified encrypted backups, import validation, and checksum verification.

Users are responsible for protecting computers with appropriate Windows sign-in security, limiting staff and administrator access, safeguarding manager PINs and sync keys, using trusted private networks, installing operating-system updates, protecting exported files, and maintaining verified backups.

No software, local database, network connection, backup, printed document, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

User and Organization Choices

The organization operating the auction controls the information entered into the app and how it is used. Authorized users can:

  • Choose what bidder, consignor, lot, staff, and auction information to enter
  • Edit or remove supported local records, subject to financial and audit safeguards
  • Record or withdraw communication consent and choose whether to prepare customer messages
  • Respond to access requests with a portable JSON export of a selected customer's information
  • Anonymize eligible archived customers after confirming legal retention obligations
  • Choose whether to import data or export reports, receipts, analytics, accounting schedules, statements, audit history, backups, or closeout packages
  • Choose whether to start or stop local-network synchronization
  • Assign staff roles and deactivate accounts
  • Delete exported files and backups from locations under their control
  • Uninstall the app and remove remaining application data according to Windows procedures

People whose information is entered into the app should direct requests to the auction company or organization operating the relevant Lazy Lions database, because CoreNucApps does not normally possess that locally stored information.

Children's Privacy

CoreNuc Auction App — Lazy Lions Edition is intended for organizations and adults who operate auctions. It is not directed to children under 13, and CoreNucApps does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app or website.

International Users

The app is designed to store auction information locally on computers selected by the user. If users export, back up, synchronize, upload, or share data using third-party services, that information may be processed in locations determined by those users and services.

Organizations using the app are responsible for determining which privacy, recordkeeping, tax, consumer-protection, employment, or other laws apply to their auctions and records.
