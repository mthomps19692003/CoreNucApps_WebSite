About the App

CoreNuc Poker League Manager is built for home poker hosts, league organizers, charity events, and tournament directors. It keeps players, tables, blinds, entries, rebuys, add-ons, payouts, displays, results, and season standings together in one Windows application.

Whether you are running a casual poker night, a recurring league event, or a structured multi-table tournament, the app supports the complete flow: build the structure, welcome players, verify the room, run live play, settle results, and share the finish.

Tournament night at a glance

The player-facing display keeps the clock, current and upcoming blinds, event status, and director controls easy to read while play is moving.

What's New in Version 2.3

Six Tournament Formats

Run Standard, Fixed Bounty, Progressive Knockout, Mystery Bounty, Satellite, and Charity events with format-aware tracking.

RSVP and Check-In

Track invited, going, waitlisted, declined, and checked-in players before the first hand is dealt.

Player Companion

Print a QR code players can scan for their seat, blinds, average stack, High Hand, bounty board, payouts, and standings.

Smart Tournament Builder

Build a blind structure around the desired duration, field size, starting stack, pace, breaks, and color-up points.

Tournament-Night Preflight

Verify blinds, seating, payouts, chips, Phone Control, TV display, sound, and backups before starting play.

Professional Presentation

Apply finished display themes and league branding, with English, Spanish, or French player-display headings and selectable currency.

Post-Game Recaps

Create shareable image and PDF recaps with results, prizes, High Hand, knockouts, entries, prize pool, and season standings.

Recovery and Backups

Recover the complete live tournament state after a restart, backed by automatic safety backups.

Phone and TV Control

Use secured Phone Control and a same-network TV Browser Display while the desktop application remains the source of truth.
