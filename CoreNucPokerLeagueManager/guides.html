Guided Tournament Workflows

Four practical walkthroughs cover the complete tournament night. The in-app guided tour and Help Center provide the same guidance while you work.

1Build the Tournament

  1. Create or load a game and choose its league and season.
  2. Use Smart Tournament Builder to generate blinds for the desired length and pace.
  3. Choose Standard, Fixed Bounty, PKO, Mystery Bounty, Satellite, or Charity.
  4. Add players, payouts, chips, and seating, then save the game.

2Welcome Players

  1. Open Player Check-In and record each RSVP.
  2. Check in arrivals and confirm table assignments.
  3. Start Browser Display and print the Player Companion QR handout.
  4. Players scan the code to follow their seat, blinds, standings, and payouts.

3Run Tournament Night

  1. Open Tournament-Night Preflight and clear required checks.
  2. Test the TV, sound, Phone Control, and pairing QR.
  3. Start the Tournament Display and record player actions as they happen.
  4. Use Undo when needed; automatic backups and live recovery protect progress.

4Share the Finish

  1. Complete settlement and confirm standings and prizes.
  2. Review High Hand, knockout leader, entries, rebuys, add-ons, and prize pool.
  3. Open the updated season leaderboard.
  4. Export the polished recap as an image or PDF to share with the league.
