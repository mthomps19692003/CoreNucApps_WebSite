Explore CoreNuc Poker League Manager version 2.3 from game setup, formats, player check-in, and preflight through the live display, Player Companion, and post-game recap.

Latest Additions

The gallery now accompanies version 2.3, which adds six tournament formats, RSVP and check-in, Smart Tournament Builder, Tournament-Night Preflight, the QR Player Companion, display themes and league branding, live-state recovery, and shareable post-game recaps. The featured Tournament Display is now also shown on the updated product page and the CoreNucApps home page.

Version 2.3 Game Setup

What This Shows

  • League, season, and game type at the top of the active event.
  • Ready checks for players, seating, blinds, tournament format, chips, payouts, display, and branding.
  • Direct actions for Player Check-In, Tournament Preflight, Player QR printing, and Display Studio.
  • A clear final readiness state before starting the tournament.

Tournament Formats And Entry Rules

What This Shows

  • Progressive Knockout selected with starting bounty and cash percentage.
  • Tournament currency and player-display language settings.
  • Club Hub status and an explanation of the selected format.
  • Selected buy-in, rebuy, and add-on options plus access to Player Check-In.

Player RSVP And Check-In

What This Shows

  • A live count of checked-in and confirmed players.
  • Going and waitlisted RSVP states.
  • Table and seat assignments beside each player.
  • Printable arrival list and reversible check-in actions.

Tournament-Night Preflight

What This Shows

  • Required checks for blinds, players, seating, payouts, chips, and safety backup.
  • Phone Control, TV Browser Display, and sound readiness.
  • One-click TV, sound, and pairing QR tests.
  • An overall Ready to Play decision with direct tournament start.

Version 2.3 Tournament Display

What This Shows

  • Large current-round clock, blinds, and ante with the next round visible below.
  • Remaining players, average stack, and prize-pool status.
  • Current High Hand and live bounty leaders.
  • Recent actions plus direct Player Actions, admin controls, and timer control.

Player Companion

What This Shows

  • A printable QR handout for the local tournament address.
  • Player selection with personal table, seat, and check-in state.
  • Current and upcoming blinds, remaining field, and average stack.
  • High Hand and personal bounty information in a phone-friendly layout.

Post-Game Recap

What This Shows

  • Tournament champion and event summary totals.
  • Final standings with prizes, points, and bounties.
  • High Hand winner and knockout leader.
  • Updated season leaderboard plus Share Image and Print PDF actions.

Game Setup

What This Shows

  • High-level counts for players, prize pool, blind rounds, and tables.
  • Setup shortcuts for players, blinds, buy-ins, and tables.
  • A current game checklist showing whether players, rounds, chips, prizes, and tables are ready.
  • Action buttons for starting the game, opening game details, and changing settings.

Tournament Display

What This Shows

  • A large round timer with current round name and clock status.
  • Current small blind, big blind, and ante values.
  • Controls for player actions, undo, full screen, and advancing to the next round.
  • Show or Hide Pairing Code controls and Player Actions that remain available beside Admin Controls.
  • A recent actions panel that helps hosts spot and correct mistakes quickly.

Player Actions

What This Shows

  • Active players with quick buttons for rebuys, add-ons, bust outs, and receipts.
  • Busted players with finish position badges and restore controls.
  • Support for correcting tournament-night mistakes without leaving the focused action panel.
  • Clear player status information so hosts can keep the game moving.

TV Browser Display

What This Shows

  • Steps for choosing which screens appear on the TV display.
  • A cycle delay setting that controls how long each TV screen stays visible.
  • A local network URL for opening the display on a TV or another computer.
  • A preview of the current TV screen, next screen, and delay timing.

Tables And Seating

What This Shows

  • Four tournament tables shown as separate table cards.
  • Seat numbers and player names organized visually inside each table.
  • Balance status for each table and a consolidation target when players need to be moved.
  • A layout built for scanning during live room management.

Player Stats

What This Shows

  • Season leaderboard totals for games, players, prize paid, and knockouts.
  • Ranked player results with score, wins, and net result.
  • A league or season selector for narrowing the standings.
  • An export action for creating shareable reports from the current stats.
  • Player career history and an option to correct totals for a completed tournament.

Settlement

What This Shows

  • Settlement totals for gross collected, prize pool, and rake or fees.
  • Player finish positions, payout amounts, and points earned.
  • A clear review point before the tournament is finalized.
  • Winner totals that include buy-ins, rebuys, and add-ons.
  • An export option for saving settlement details after the game.

Game Details

What This Shows

  • Players and seating assignments with table badges.
  • Upcoming rounds, blinds, antes, and break information.
  • A two-column Notes presentation with automatic refresh behavior.
  • A matching, responsive Game Details view for the TV Browser Display.
