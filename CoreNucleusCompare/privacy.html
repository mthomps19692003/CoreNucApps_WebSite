Core Nucleus Compare

Privacy

The app is designed to inspect files and folders directly on your Windows computer without sending their contents to CoreNucApps.

Information the app handles

Core Nucleus Compare reads the files, folders, metadata, archives, and supported document contents that you explicitly select. It also stores local preferences, comparison profiles, operation history, cached fingerprints, logs, and scheduling information needed to provide its features.

Where information is stored

Application settings and working data are stored in the app's local Windows application-data location. Enterprise administrators may provide a local policy file through the shared Windows program-data location.

Network use

Normal comparison work does not require a CoreNucApps account or transfer selected file contents to CoreNucApps. Any network access caused by opening an external link, comparing a network location, or using Windows services is initiated through the destination or service you choose.

File operations

Comparison is read-only. Copy, move, rename, recycle, and delete features are separate actions that require your direction. Folder monitoring only marks results as stale; it does not automatically rescan or change files.

Sharing and retention

CoreNucApps does not sell your comparison data. You control the source files and any reports you export. Local settings, caches, logs, and reports remain until you remove them through the app, Windows, or your chosen storage location.
