The first complete home-management workspace brings upkeep, records, projects, spending, and preparedness together.

Included in this release

  • A home dashboard for maintenance reminders, budget, actual spend, and document totals.
  • HVAC and pool maintenance records with reusable items and recurrence details.
  • Appliance warranties, property information, rooms, and a digital home binder.
  • Home-improvement projects, estimates, materials, priorities, budgets, and costs.
  • Utility tracking and maintenance-intelligence profiles.
  • Disaster preparedness plus move-in and move-out checklists.
  • Local inspection analysis, predictive maintenance, and “Ask My House” tools.
  • PDF and spreadsheet export options for supported records.
  • Preferences, contextual help, icons, tooltips, and responsive layouts.

Storage and privacy

Home records are stored locally in SQLite. Built-in intelligence uses local rules and records. The optional hurricane-context request uses the public OpenFEMA API.

Platform targets

The project targets Windows, Android, iOS, and Mac Catalyst through .NET MAUI. Availability and packaging may vary by platform and distribution channel.
