Home Maintenance Manager

Privacy

Your home records are stored on your device and remain under your control.

Information you enter

The app can store property details, maintenance records, vendor notes, budgets, costs, utilities, room information, projects, preparedness and moving checklists, warranties, and document records that you choose to add.

Local storage

Structured records are saved in a SQLite database within the app's local data area. Imported property documents are copied into the app's local document storage. Preferences are stored using the operating system's application-preference service.

AI features

Inspection analysis, predictive maintenance, and “Ask My House” features use built-in rules and your locally stored records. They do not send those records to an external AI provider.

Optional network feature

When you request North Carolina hurricane context, the app contacts the public OpenFEMA API and receives disaster-declaration information. The request is for public FEMA data and does not need to include your home records.

Exports and external links

You control any PDF or spreadsheet exports, saved files, and external material links. Once shared or opened outside the app, those items are governed by the destination and the choices you make.

Sharing and deletion

CoreNucApps does not sell your home records. You can edit or delete records in the app and remove application data through your device's operating-system controls.
